WHAT YOU NEED TO DO
Being Future-Oriented
Example to anticipate OPPORTUNITIES using the "if A, then B" technique:
− If the road to Nairobi is upgraded at the end of the year...
...I will look for suppliers in the city who offer a lower price or higher quality.
...I will join an association of entrepreneurs
...I will plan to offer my products in Nairobi as the transport is faster and cheaper
Example to anticipate BARRIERS using the "if A, then B" technique:
If the electricity company cuts the power again next week...
...I will use my own generator or share one with others.
...I'll have a machine at hand that doesn't need electricity for this.
...I will have as many spare parts in stock as I need to produce until the power comes back
on.

Being Future-Oriented
Example to anticipate long-term CONSEQUENCES
Karobia runs a kiosk near a school. His customers are
mainly students and teachers. He’s been successfully
running his business for a few years. A few weeks before
the start of the school year, Karobia learns about a new
restaurant opening right next to his kiosk. He’s afraid
students and teachers will buy from the new competitor,
so he decides to reduce the prices of almost all his
items.
In fact, Karobia gains some customers. But at the end of
the school year, he finds he’s made a huge loss.
What did Karobia do wrong?
