EXERCISE: Rephrasing the Objectives
After several jobs in the food industry, Frank wants to start a catering service. He has
drawn up a plan in which he identifies an objective for his business: ‘I want to become
the most famous restaurant in my region ’.
WS
20
Instruction:
Form groups of three (03) people to work on this exercise. Apply the
SMART-PI principles. Answer the following questions:
1. What criteria (SMART-PI) does Frank’s objective meet?
2. Does Frank’s objective show enough personal initiative?
3. How could he reformulate his objective to meet the SMART-PI
criteria?
