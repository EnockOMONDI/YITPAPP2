7
1. Treat each other with respect
2. Provide constructive feedback on the task, not on
the person
3. Start and end with a positive point – place any
negative feedback in he middle (sandwich rule)
4. But don't forget the negative points: these
highlight the possibilities for improvement
5. Always see mistakes as a learning opportunity
and share these with others as well

Training
8
1. See feedback as the way to learn and improve
2. Listen carefully: Always ask questions if you haven’t
understood something
3. Take notes: This makes it easier for you to
remember the points made by your colleagues
4. Don’t react defensively, accept the opinion of the
person giving you their feedback and thank them
for helping you
5. Decide how to act on the feedback. Think about
how you can implement any necessary changes
