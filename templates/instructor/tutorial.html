    👋 Scenario Introduction: Meet Beryl Omondi

    BO
    Beryl Omondi

    Course Instructor • Youth Development Specialist • 8 years experience

    In this comprehensive tutorial, we'll follow Beryl Omondi, an experienced Course Instructor at YITP, as she creates a new unit called "Personal Initiative" for the Youth Impact Training Course.

    Learning Objectives

    By following Beryl's journey, you'll learn how to:

    • Navigate the YITP LMS instructor dashboard
    • Create and structure course modules and lessons
    • Upload and manage multimedia content
    • Design engaging assessments and quizzes
    • Monitor student progress and analytics
    • Utilize advanced content management features
    Scenario Context

    Course: Youth Impact Training Course

    New Unit: Personal Initiative

    Target Audience: Young professionals aged 18-25

    Duration: 2 weeks (4 lessons)

    Content Types: PowerPoint presentations, PDF documents, video materials, interactive quizzes

    💡 Best Practices & Tips

    Content Creation Tips
    • Use YITP-branded styles consistently
    • Keep lessons focused and concise
    • Include interactive elements in every lesson
    • Test all multimedia before publishing
    • Provide clear learning objectives
    Student Engagement Tips
    • Respond to messages within 24 hours
    • Monitor analytics regularly
    • Provide constructive quiz feedback
    • Use varied content types
    • Encourage peer interaction
    Quality Assurance

    Always preview your content from a student's perspective before publishing. Check that:

    • All links and media work correctly
    • Quiz questions have correct answers marked
    • Sequential progression is properly configured
    • Content follows YITP style guidelines

    🔧 Troubleshooting Guide

    Common Issues and Solutions

    File Upload Problems

    Issue: File upload fails or times out

    Solution: Check file size (max 100MB) and format compatibility

    Quiz Not Appearing

    Issue: Students can't see quiz after lesson

    Solution: Verify quiz is published and associated with correct lesson

    Content Formatting Issues

    Issue: Content doesn't display with YITP styling

    Solution: Use CKEditor styles dropdown to apply YITP-branded formatting

    Student Progress Not Updating

    Issue: Analytics don't show recent student activity

    Solution: Analytics update every 15 minutes; check again later
