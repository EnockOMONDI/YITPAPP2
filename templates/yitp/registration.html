Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about registration, payment, and the program.

Who is eligible for the Youth Impact Training Programme?

The program is designed for ambitious young people aged 15-25 who are passionate about making a positive impact in their communities. No prior experience is required - just enthusiasm and commitment to personal growth.

What does the 12-week program include?

The program includes:

  • Weekly interactive training sessions
  • One-on-one mentorship with industry experts
  • Practical project work and community engagement
  • Access to our global network of young leaders
  • Official certification upon completion
Can I pay in installments?

Yes! We offer flexible payment options including 2 installments of KES 2,500 ($20) each. You can pay the first installment during registration and the second installment within 4 weeks of starting the program.

When does the next cohort start?

Our next cohort begins on January 15, 2025. We run multiple cohorts throughout the year, so if you miss this one, the following cohort will start in April 2025. Early registration is recommended as spaces are limited.

Is the program conducted online or in-person?

We offer a hybrid approach combining online sessions for flexibility and in-person workshops for hands-on experience. This allows participants from different locations to join while still providing valuable face-to-face interaction and networking opportunities.
