Who We Are

Our Foundation

The Youth Impact Training Programme (YITP) is a short, practical training programme that prepares young people for success in business, employment, and personal development.

  • Training in 21st-century knowledge & soft skills
  • Business mentorship from industry experts
  • Opportunities for apprenticeship
  • Expert guidance on youth challenges

Our Origin & Mission

The Youth Impact Training Programme (YITP) was founded as part of the Narrow Passes' Youth Impact Forum, an initiative that helps young people solve real-world challenges under Success Afrika.

Our Mission:

  • Empower young people through training, mentorship, and networking
  • Help youth become problem solvers, entrepreneurs, and leaders

Our Impact & Initiatives

Youth Impact Media Initiative (YIMI)

The Youth Impact Training Programme (YITP) is part of the Youth Impact Media Initiative (YIMI), which extends our mission through media and partnerships.

Our Flagship Products:

  • The Youth Impact Show – Engaging discussions on youth Agendas
  • The Youth Impact Forum – A space for learning & networking
  • The Youth Impact Summit – Large-scale leadership & career event
  • The Youth Impact Training Programme (YITP) – Our core training initiative
  • Youth Impact Awards – Celebrating youth who transform communities and shape the world

Our Contribution to Global Goals

Supporting UN Sustainable Development Goals

The Youth Impact Training Programme (YITP) actively supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) through our comprehensive training and empowerment initiatives.

SDG 1: No Poverty

Helping youth gain financial independence through skills training and entrepreneurship opportunities.

SDG 5: Gender Equality

Ensuring equal access to training, mentorship, and leadership opportunities for all genders.

SDG 8: Decent Work

Preparing young professionals and entrepreneurs for sustainable economic growth.
