Who We Are

An international organisation solving the global unemployment crisis — one youth at a time.

Youth Impact Global targets the critical gap between education and employment. We partner with young and middle-aged people — helping them transition from college, university, or self-discovery into employment or self-employment with an informed mindset and a high success rate.

Our goal is to be the platform of change for students and youth empowerment programmes globally — building changemakers through soft skills, life skills, and green economy readiness.

Our Objectives
  • Make youth and junior professionals partners in solving the unemployment crisis — creating entrepreneurs and securing sustainable income.
  • Mentor young people and instil self-awareness on purpose, mindset, and personal initiative.
  • Build changemakers through soft skills and life skills training for lasting success.
  • Equip youth with green economy skills that open doors in solar, e-mobility, climate-smart agriculture, and sustainable industries.
