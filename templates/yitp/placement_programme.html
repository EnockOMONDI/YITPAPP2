Placement Opportunities Programme - POP

Bridging the gap between graduation and career success through strategic placements and professional immersion.

After Training, What's Next?

Completing the
empowerment cycle.

The Youth Impact Global is proud to be running the Placement Opportunities Programme - POP, an initiative that aims to help young people access opportunities within and outside our network. POP aims to create informed and valuable employees with an entrepreneurial mindset to thrive in the corporate world. We solve the unemployment crisis through a strategic three-tier placement model.

Internships (3–6 Months)

Short-term skill testing and workplace immersion focused on practical experience.

Strategic Apprenticeships

6–12 month structured "earn-while-you-learn" models for technical roles.

Job Placement

Permanent roles for high-growth local sectors and international labour placement.
