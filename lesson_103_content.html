  • o What is life's purpose? 7 Questions for Clarity:
  • 1. What makes you feel most alive?
  • 2. What problem in the world gets you most emotionally charged?
  • 3. What are you naturally good at?
  • 4. What is your life's most meaningful event or experience?
  • 5. What would you do if you had zero fear?
  • 6. Who is your idol, living or dead, and why?
  • 7. What do you want to be your enduring legacy?
  • Answer the foregoing questions sincerely, for therein lies the discovery of your purpose.
  • o Difference between goals, vision, and purpose.
  • 1. Purpose begets vision
  • 2. Vision begets goals
  • 3. Purpose: The reason for which something is done or created or for which
  • something exists. Your purpose is the reason for your being.
  • 4. Vision: An act or power of anticipating which will or may come to be –
  • giving direction for the outcome of goals and purpose
  • 5. Goals: specific, desired outcomes that guide actions and effort towards a
  • particular predetermined end
  • o Purpose-driven lives: Gandhi's nonviolence, Mandela's fight for justice,
  • Mother Teresa's compassion.
  • Gandhi, Mandela and Mother Teresa are shining examples of lives well lived with
  • purpose. Gandhi left his law practice behind and focused on liberating Asia and
  • Africa from the yolks of colonial rule. He often took expeditions to South Africa
  • to fellowship with the who were engaged in freedom struggle.
  • His crown jewel of endeavor came in the form of relentless non-violent resistance
  • against British rule of his nation, India. He summed it all in the following thought.
  • "The main purpose of life is to live rightly, think rightly, act rightly. The soul
  • must languish when we give all our thought to the body." Mahatma Gandhi
  • Mandela's purposeful find for the liberation of his nation landed him in much
  • trouble, and he was imprisoned and even labelled a terrorist among other things. It
  • was his purpose that fueled his staying power. And he stayed the course until he
  • birthed a rainbow nation out of Apartheid.
  • "For to be free is not merely to cast off one's chains, but to live in a way that
  • respects and enhances the freedom of others." – Nelson Mandela
  • Mother Teresa's life in Calcutta and beyond touched the world in a manner most
